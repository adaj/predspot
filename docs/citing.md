# Citing Predspot

If you use Predspot in your research, please cite the thesis and the paper
that introduced the framework:

> Araujo, A., & Cacho, N. (2019). *Predspot: Predicting crime hotspots with
> machine learning*. Master's thesis, Universidade Federal do Rio Grande do
> Norte (UFRN), Natal, Brazil.

> Araújo, A., Cacho, N., Bezerra, L., Vieira, C., & Borges, J. (2018). Towards
> a crime hotspot detection framework for patrol planning. In *2018 IEEE 20th
> International Conference on High Performance Computing and Communications;
> IEEE 16th International Conference on Smart City; IEEE 4th International
> Conference on Data Science and Systems (HPCC/SmartCity/DSS)* (pp. 1256-1263).
> IEEE.

```bibtex
@mastersthesis{araujo2019predspot,
```
